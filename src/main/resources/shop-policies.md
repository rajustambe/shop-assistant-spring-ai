# ShopCo Customer Policies

## Returns and Exchanges
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unused and in their
original packaging with all tags attached. To start a return, open your order in the account
page and select "Request Return". Once we receive and inspect the item, refunds are issued to
the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Opened consumables, innerwear, and
personalized items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Exchanges for a different size or
colour are free of charge for the first exchange on any order.

## Shipping and Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days across India. Shipping is free on all orders above
Rs.499; orders below that carry a flat Rs.49 shipping fee. Customers in metro cities (Bangalore,
Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) can choose next-day delivery at checkout for
an extra Rs.99. We currently ship only within India and do not deliver to international addresses.
A tracking link is emailed and sent over SMS once the order is dispatched.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled for a full refund any time before its status changes to SHIPPED. Once
an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under
the standard return policy. To cancel, go to the order page and click "Cancel Order"; the refund
is processed immediately to the original payment method.

## Warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects. Warranty
claims require the original invoice, which is available as a PDF in your order history. The
warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, or damage from unauthorized repairs.
For a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a short description of the fault.

## Payments
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets. Cash on Delivery (COD)
is available for orders under Rs.5000. EMI options are offered on orders above Rs.3000 for select
banks. All online payments are processed over a secure PCI-DSS compliant gateway, and we never
store your full card number on our servers.

## Loyalty and Coupons
Members of the ShopCo Rewards programme earn 2 points for every Rs.100 spent, and points can be
redeemed at a rate of 100 points to Rs.50. Only one coupon code can be applied per order, and
coupons cannot be combined with clearance-sale pricing. Reward points do not expire as long as
the account remains active with at least one purchase every 18 months.
